# 10 Scorecard and Operating Rhythm

Volume alone is an inadequate measure. A team can publish more and become less authoritative.

## Balanced scorecard

| Area | What to measure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Production efficiency | Brief-to-publication time, on-time delivery, revision rounds | `content/*/status.md` dates and change logs |
| Editorial quality | Factual error rate, style-guide compliance, substantive editing required | `review.md` verdicts, corrections log |
| Taste and selection | Quality of shortlisted venues, distinctiveness, audience relevance | Classification mix, monthly sample review |
| Search performance | Qualified organic traffic, rankings, impressions, engagement | Search Console, Semrush, DataForSEO |
| Authority | Expert contributions, credible references, destination coverage, returning readership | Asset records, dossier coverage, analytics |
| Listing health | Verification freshness, completeness, broken links, update compliance | `knowledge/listings/` |
| Business contribution | Qualified enquiries, useful referrals, partner outcomes, assisted conversions | CRM and analytics, supplied by the business |

**Set baselines before targets.** The first monthly review records baselines in `knowledge/scorecard.md`; targets are set from the second quarter.

**Incentives.** Never reward output volume or keyword placement alone. Those incentives produce generic content, unnecessary articles and superficial optimisation.

## Rhythm

| Cadence | Meeting | Purpose | Run with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly | Production review (short, decision-oriented) | Decisions only: blockers, approvals, agenda changes from the intelligence brief. Nobody reads out the tracker; `content/PIPELINE.md` is read beforehand. | `/ww-intel-brief` + `content/PIPELINE.md` |
| Monthly | Taste and performance review | Content quality, search performance, listing gaps, emerging trends, distinctiveness of recent commissions; monthly editorial quality audit | `/ww-monthly-review` |
| Quarterly | Authority and destination review | Destination priorities, outdated content, industry relationships, content gaps, overall editorial direction | `/ww-quarterly-review` |

### Weekly production review agenda (15 to 20 minutes)

1. Decisions needed this week (from `PIPELINE.md` items marked blocked or awaiting approval).
2. Intelligence brief recommendations: commission, monitor, update or disregard.
3. Any correction or escalation.
4. Owners confirmed for the coming week.
