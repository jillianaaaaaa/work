## 1. Journey stage, intent and H1

- **Journey stage:** Comparison, moving into Selection (agrees with `status.md`). The reader has a destination or region in mind and is comparing properties.
- **Search intent:** informational with commercial investigation. The live SERP for the primary keyword is mixed: US directory and local-listing pages, a local pack, two venue listicles and one event-by-event editorial page. No ranking page explains how to assess a property for a multi-day wedding, which is the gap this asset fills.
- **Primary purpose:** become the primary page for the new strategic topic "choosing a venue for a multi-day wedding weekend", supporting `/plan-your-destination-wedding` and feeding `/destination-wedding-packages`, `/wonderland-concierge` and the regional hubs.
- **Recommended slug:** `/expert-advice/how-to-choose-a-wedding-weekend-venue` (matches the live `/expert-advice/` pattern; keeps "itinerary" out of the URL so it does not compete with the live itinerary article).

**H1 options (broad, never audience-limiting)**
1. How to Choose a Wedding Weekend Venue That Hosts Every Occasion (recommended)
2. Wedding Weekend Venues: How to Find a Property for the Whole Celebration
3. Choosing a Venue for a Multi-Day Wedding: What to Look For and What to Ask

All three lead with the primary pattern, contain no "X, not Y" framing and avoid region or audience qualifiers in the H1 (regions appear in the worked examples instead).

**Common denominators to mirror (in a WW version)**
- Separate ceremony and reception spaces called out per venue.
- On-site accommodation for couple, family and guests as a headline criterion.
- A suggested weekend shape (schedule) and an FAQ.

**Content gaps worth filling**
1. No ranking page defines exclusive use versus partial buy-out, minimum nights or whether other hotel guests remain on property.
2. No one counts distinct settings per occasion (and a wet-weather alternative for each).
3. No one relates room inventory to guest list, or explains overflow stays and transfer times between events.
4. Where the legal ceremony sits relative to the estate is absent everywhere.
5. Ranking pages are almost entirely US-regional and listing-led; there is no international, verified, dated set of worked examples (Italy plus at least one other region).
6. Nothing frames guest hospitality over several days; the SERP and PAA lean on cost.

**AI Overview.** DataForSEO flags an AI Overview at position 1 for both "wedding weekend venues" and "how to choose a destination wedding venue", and AI-expanded PAA answers, but the content loads asynchronously and was not returned. **Request for Jillian:** please share AI Overview screenshots for "wedding weekend venues", "wedding venues with accommodation" and "exclusive use wedding venue" (US and AU if possible) so we can see which sources and fact types Google cites. Until then, the brief should make each H2 open with a quotable one-sentence answer and carry verified numbers (rooms, settings, capacities) with last-verified dates.
