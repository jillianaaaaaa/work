# Wedded Wonderland Editorial Handbook

The handbook writers consult while working. Each chapter ends in something the team does, not just something it believes.

| Chapter | Use it when |
|---|---|
| [01 Philosophy](01-philosophy.md) | Deciding whether anything deserves to be published |
| [02 Team and ownership](02-team-and-ownership.md) | Working out who owns a function or stage |
| [03 Taste system](03-taste-system.md) | Assessing a venue, hotel, supplier, destination or trend; assigning Signature, Specialist or Emerging |
| [04 Trend desk](04-trend-desk.md) | Monitoring, validating and commissioning trends; the weekly intelligence brief |
| [05 Destination authority](05-destination-authority.md) | Building region, country, area and venue knowledge; dossiers |
| [06 Workflow](06-workflow.md) | Moving an asset through the seven stages; review tiers |
| [07 Style guide](07-style-guide.md) | Writing, editing, images, partner claims, corrections |
| [08 Listing standard](08-listing-standard.md) | Writing or updating a listing |
| [09 SEO and AI discoverability](09-seo-ai-discoverability.md) | Planning topics, links and refreshes |
| [10 Scorecard and rhythm](10-scorecard-and-rhythm.md) | Measuring the operation; weekly, monthly and quarterly reviews |

Article mechanics (structure, banned words, links, photos, CTA block, pre-delivery checklist) live in the `wedded-wonderland-articles` skill. The handbook does not repeat them.

**The defining principle.** Operations are standardised. Editorial judgement is not outsourced to the standardisation.
