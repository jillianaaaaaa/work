# Knowledge Base

The team's cumulative memory. Research is done once and filed here so every later asset starts from what we already know.

| Path | Contents | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| `dossiers/<region>/<country>/<area>.md` | Destination dossiers, mirroring the region, country, area hierarchy (`_region.md` and `_country.md` for the upper levels) | ww-research-intelligence |
| `listings/<slug>.md` | Venue, hotel and supplier records: structured facts plus editorial copy | ww-research-intelligence (facts), ww-writer (copy) |
| `intelligence/YYYY-MM-DD.md` | Weekly intelligence briefs | ww-research-intelligence |
| `topic-map.md` | One primary page per strategic topic and its supporting pages | ww-seo-strategist |
| `performance-log.md` | Published assets, target queries, refresh dates, learnings | ww-seo-strategist |
| `reviews/` | Monthly and quarterly review reports | ww-editorial-director |
| `scorecard.md` | Baselines and monthly and quarterly scorecard results | ww-editorial-director |

Rules: every fact has a source and a verified date; unconfirmed details stay in open questions; declines are recorded with reasons.
