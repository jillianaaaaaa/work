# 01 Editorial Philosophy

The philosophy comes before the workflow. It is the shared understanding of what Wedded Wonderland considers exceptional, and it governs every listing, destination guide, trend report, partner feature and caption.

## The five principles as operating standards

| Principle | What it means | The operational test |
|---|---|---|
| **Taste over trend adoption** | Know what is gaining attention; never publish something because it is popular. | Can we state its design integrity, cultural relevance, originality and lasting appeal? If the only reason is reach, decline or monitor. |
| **Expertise over aspiration alone** | Imagery attracts attention; knowledge earns authority. | Does the piece contain local knowledge, accurate details, an informed recommendation and practical intelligence the reader can act on? |
| **Distinctiveness over excess** | Luxury is shown through specificity, discernment and access. | Remove every adjective. Is there still a reason to care? Is "exclusive" or "luxury" doing work that a verified detail should do? |
| **Editorial integrity over promotional copy** | Every venue or brand earns its place. Partners meet the same standard. | Is there a stated reason this is featured? Is any commercial relationship disclosed? Would we feature it without the partnership? |
| **A connected view of celebrations and travel** | Weddings, destinations, hotels, fashion, hospitality, wellness and milestone travel form one considered world. | Does the piece connect to the relevant destination hub, related celebrations or onward travel where that helps the reader? |

## Applied example: approving a venue listing

A listing is not approved because the property is beautiful. It must answer:

1. What makes it distinctive?
2. What kind of celebration does it accommodate?
3. What does its location contribute?
4. Why does it merit the attention of Wedded Wonderland's audience?

If any answer is missing, the listing returns to research or is declined.

## The three questions (every asset, every tier)

1. Why does this deserve to be featured?
2. What can our reader learn here that they would not get from a generic search result?
3. Does this strengthen Wedded Wonderland's authority and point of view?

These are answered in writing in every `review.md`. A weak answer means the piece is not ready.

## The ambition

Not the publication that covers the most destinations or trends. The publication whose selection carries weight, whose recommendations are useful and whose perspective is recognisable before the reader sees the byline. Efficiency exists to protect quality.
