# 04 The Trend Intelligence Desk

A trend intelligence desk identifies movements early, understands their origins and decides which deserve coverage. It is not a calendar of whatever is trending.

## Four stages

1. **Observe.** Monitor hotel openings, destination developments, designers, architecture, hospitality, travel behaviour, fashion collections, cultural shifts and emerging creative talent.
2. **Interpret.** Identify the underlying change. A new aesthetic? A shift in how couples travel? Changing guest expectations? A different attitude to celebration?
3. **Validate.** Distinguish sustained movement from isolated social-media attention. Require multiple credible, independent signals, direct sources and relevant industry perspectives.
4. **Commission.** Develop an original angle, determine audience value and choose the format: feature, destination guide, listing update, interview, social story, or nothing.

### Validation threshold

| Signal strength | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| One source, or social attention only | Monitor |
| Two or more independent credible signals, no clear angle yet | Monitor, with a question to answer |
| Multiple signals and an original angle with audience value | Commission |
| Affects facts in a live listing or dossier | Update |
| Fails taste criteria, or no reader value | Disregard, with the reason logged |

### Example of the difference an angle makes

Generic: another article on the popularity of intimate weddings.
Wedded Wonderland: how smaller guest lists are changing the choice of private estates, the length of destination stays, dining formats and the design of the whole guest experience.

The second shows a point of view.

## The weekly intelligence brief

Owner: `ww-research-intelligence`. Template: `templates/weekly-intelligence-brief.md`. Filed at `knowledge/intelligence/YYYY-MM-DD.md` and run with `/ww-intel-brief`.

Contents, kept concise:
- Five to ten notable developments worth monitoring.
- Two or three emerging editorial opportunities.
- One or two developments that could materially affect existing destination or venue listings.
- New properties, experts and sources to investigate.
- A clear recommendation per item: publish, monitor, update or disregard.

`ww-editorial-director` reads the brief and decides which opportunities enter the agenda. Not every observation becomes an article. Rejecting a trend is as important as identifying one.
