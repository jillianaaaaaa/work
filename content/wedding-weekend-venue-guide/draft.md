As more weddings run over three days, wedding weekend venues are judged on everything they can hold across the stay. This is Wedded Wonderland's guide to reading a destination wedding venue for the whole weekend, covering exclusive use, guest rooms and the walk between dinner and dancing, shown through four properties in England, Italy and South Africa.

## Key Takeaways

- In the United States nearly one in three weddings now spans at least three days, so the venue decision covers a whole stay.
- Exclusive use comes in tiers. Ask what stays open to the public, how many nights are required and what the buy-out leaves out.
- Count the settings. A venue suited to a three day wedding gives each occasion its own space, with a wet-weather alternative for each.
- Set beds against guests. The gap between how many can celebrate and how many can sleep on site is the most useful single test.
- Plan the movement. Walking paths, estate vehicles or coaches decide how the weekend feels between events.
- House rules on end times, minimum stays and seasonal spaces shape the days as firmly as the setting does.

## How to Choose a Venue for a Wedding Weekend

Choose a wedding weekend venue by testing whether one property can host every occasion and every guest across the stay. That means the welcome dinner, the ceremony, the celebration and the lunch the day after. Bring two figures to every venue conversation, your guest count and the number of nights, because each test that follows turns on them.

In the United States nearly one in three weddings now spans at least three days, Axios reported on 18 October 2026. Advisors surveyed for Virtuoso's 2027 Luxe Report, published in September 2026, rank celebration travel second among ultraluxe experiences, and 47 percent say they have seen an increase in ultraluxe travel, which the report defines to include exclusive use. That makes the venue a decision about the whole stay, and our [planning hub](https://weddedwonderland.com/plan-your-destination-wedding) sets out how we help couples shortlist for it.

### Why the Venue Now Carries the Whole Stay

A celebration over several days asks guests to give their time and energy, and it gives the couple more hours to host them well. Dove Cameron and Damiano David held a civil ceremony in Montalcino, then their main ceremony on the Saturday at Castiglion del Bosco, a Rosewood estate in the Val d'Orcia, with the wedding planned by Alessandra Grillo.

Vogue reported on 29 September 2026 that the dinner after the civil ceremony also returned to the estate, so a single property carried two of the weekend's occasions. For more on [how a Tuscan celebration unfolds over several days](https://weddedwonderland.com/expert-advice/what-is-a-tuscany-wedding-actually-like), our Tuscany guide walks through the format.

### The Five Tests We Apply to Every Property

We read every property against five tests, covering how much of it you can take, how many distinct settings it offers, where each guest sleeps, how people move between events and which house rules shape the days. The four properties that follow, in Hampshire, Tuscany, Palermo and the Cape Winelands, each show one or more of these tests at work. For the shape of the days themselves, our [wedding weekend schedule guide](https://weddedwonderland.com/a-wedding-weekend-itinerary-for-guest-experience) sets out each occasion in turn.

## Exclusive Use and Partial Buy-Outs at a Wedding Weekend Venue

Exclusive use gives your party a venue, or a defined part of it, to itself for the dates you book, and each property draws that line in a different place. Before you compare settings, ask which spaces stay open to the public, how many nights the venue requires and what the buy-out leaves out.

### What a Full Buy-Out Covers at Heckfield Place

Heckfield Place sells exclusive use of its Georgian house and 400-acre (about 160-hectare) Hampshire estate in three tiers, per its Events 2025 brochure, the latest edition linked from its website. Under A Key to the House and Walled Gardens, members still use The Bothy spa, and only the third tier, A Key to Heckfield, gives total privacy. Every tier carries a two-night minimum, and the estate's farm sits outside the buy-out at all three levels.

Those tiers make Heckfield a clear reference point when you weigh an English country house against estates further afield. Our [guide to marrying in Europe](https://weddedwonderland.com/destinations/europe) covers the regions couples most often compare it with.

## A Setting for Each Occasion of a Multi-Day Wedding

A venue suited to a wedding weekend gives each occasion its own setting, with the welcome party, the wedding day and the farewell brunch each held somewhere distinct and a wet-weather alternative for each. Count the spaces a property can give you, then assign every occasion to its day and setting with our [wedding day timeline](https://weddedwonderland.com/expert-advice/wedding-day-timeline).

A property with places to pass unscheduled hours, such as gardens, a pool, a spa or walking paths, lets the free time between occasions feel hosted. Before you add [a welcome party](https://weddedwonderland.com/expert-advice/do-you-need-a-wedding-welcome-party) or begin [designing each event as an experience](https://weddedwonderland.com/how-to-turn-your-destination-wedding-event-into-an-experience), confirm the venue has a separate space ready for it.

## Where Every Guest Sleeps at a Wedding Weekend Venue

The easiest wedding weekend venue to host is one where most of your guests can sleep on site, with rooms nearby and arranged transfers for everyone else. The most useful single test of wedding venues with accommodation is the gap between how many guests can celebrate on the property and how many can sleep there. Ask for both figures, then ask where the rest of the party stays, how far away that is and who arranges it.

At the resorts they cover, our [destination wedding packages](https://weddedwonderland.com/destination-wedding-packages) arrange a group room block and coordinate guest travel and transfers, and the same questions apply to any property on your shortlist. In Tuscany, our guide to [how many guests a Tuscan villa can sleep](https://weddedwonderland.com/expert-advice/can-you-get-married-in-a-tuscan-villa) sets out what an exclusive-use villa can hold.

### Beds Against Guests at Heckfield Place

With exclusive use, Heckfield Place hosts up to 120 guests by day in the house, or 150 with a marquee on Circus Maximus or at Big Thumper, and 90 guests overnight, per its Events 2025 brochure. A further 28 rooms at Wellington Lodge at Highfield can be arranged for guests beyond the house, and the events team can tell you how far it sits from the main house.

For an English country-house weekend with up to 90 guests sleeping on site, Heckfield Place is the property we recommend. Above that number, the Wellington Lodge rooms and the transfers between them become part of the plan.

### When Only Part of the Party Sleeps on the Farm

Babylonstoren's wedding packages include accommodation on the farm for a small part of the party, while the Old Cellar seats 100 for dinner. For a weekend, that leaves the couple to plan where most guests sleep and how they arrive each evening, a question for the functions team before any date is held.

## Moving Guests Between Events at a Wedding Weekend Venue

Choose a venue where guests can walk between events, or where the estate runs vehicles or coaches between them, and ask how long each transfer takes. The shorter and better arranged the journey between settings, the more of the weekend guests share. Through [Wedded Concierge](https://weddedwonderland.com/wonderland-concierge) we arrange arrivals and transfers so no guest has to organise their own.

### Distances Across a 2,000-Hectare Estate at Castiglion del Bosco

Castiglion del Bosco covers 2,000 hectares (5,000 acres), so settings across the estate can sit some distance from the borgo. Each of its eleven villas comes with a Land Rover Defender or similar 4x4 for guests. Ask how the estate moves a full party between settings after dark.

### Arriving at the Property

Heckfield Place gives its journey from London as under an hour and offers Land Rovers or coaches to move guests, while Villa Igiea suggests arrival by boat for a wedding party. For a property further from a capital, ask for the nearest international airport and the transfer time before it reaches your shortlist.

## Dining Rooms and Kitchens at a Wedding Weekend Venue

A wedding weekend venue needs more than one dining room able to cater on the same weekend, so the welcome dinner and the day-after lunch can each take a different format. Ask which restaurants can be taken privately, how many each seats and where the menus draw their produce from. [A relaxed brunch the next morning](https://weddedwonderland.com/the-art-of-recovery-brunch-gen-z-approved-way) may suit a different room and kitchen from the wedding dinner.

### Three Dining Rooms at Heckfield Place

Skye Gyngell, Heckfield's culinary director, sets menus across Marle, which seats 85 inside and 40 on the terrace and is available with exclusive use only, Hearth, built around open-fire cooking, and the Dining Room, which seats 26. Heckfield describes its Home Farm as certified organic and its Market Garden as certified biodynamic, and both shape those menus. It also reports a sustainability record that includes zero waste to landfill.

### Family-Style Tables at Babylonstoren

Babylonstoren serves wedding dinners as family-style platters drawn from its own garden, which also supplies the flowers. The farm offers [the rehearsal dinner](https://weddedwonderland.com/expert-advice/rehearsal-dinner) on request, and its three restaurants, Babel, Greenhouse and Old Bakery, are worth raising for the other meals of the weekend. Ask the functions team whether any of them can be reserved for your party.

End times, minimum stays and seasonal limits decide what each day of a wedding weekend can hold, so read them before you plan a single event. ### End Times and Dance Floors

Babylonstoren's evening functions end at midnight and can run to 2am at an additional hourly fee, which may change, so confirm current terms with the venue. Its guide also rules out an outdoor dance floor unless all accommodation is taken, which ties the shape of the night to how much of the farm the party holds.

### Minimum Stays and Seasonal Spaces

Heckfield Place sets a two-night minimum for exclusive use, which holds a welcome dinner, a wedding day and [a gathering the morning after](https://weddedwonderland.com/expert-advice/the-morning-after-wedding-trend-couples-are-adding-to-their-wedding-weekend) within one stay. Its Pavilion by the Lower Lake, licensed for ceremonies of up to 30 guests, is best suited to spring, summer and autumn.

## Questions to Ask a Wedding Weekend Venue Before You Sign

Before you sign, ask each events team to confirm in writing which year's terms apply to your dates, because operators' own documents do not always agree. Heckfield's exclusive-use tiers sit in its 2025 events brochure, and Babylonstoren's wedding guide and website differ on which accommodation its packages include.

1. Which year's terms apply to our dates, and will you confirm them in writing?
2. Which rooms, houses and spaces are included, by name?
3. What stays open to other guests or members under our option?
4. Can the legal ceremony take place on site, and if not, where?
5. Which suppliers must you approve, and is an outside planner required?
6. Which dietary requirements and family needs can the kitchen and accommodation meet?
7. When must music end, and can that be extended?

## Glossary

- Exclusive use: the right to have a venue, or a defined part of it, for your party alone on the dates booked.
- Full buy-out: booking every room and space a property offers, so no other guests share it.
- Partial buy-out: booking a defined block of rooms and spaces while the rest of the property stays open.
- Minimum stay: the number of nights a venue requires for an exclusive-use booking.
- Borgo: a small historic Italian village or hamlet, often restored as a single estate.
- Licensed space: in England and Wales, a room or linked outdoor area at approved premises where a civil marriage can take place before a superintendent registrar and registrar.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do you host guests who cannot sleep at the venue?**
Block rooms at a hotel or house close to the venue and arrange timed transfers to and from every event, so no guest has to drive or organise their own way. Heckfield Place, for example, can arrange 28 rooms at Wellington Lodge at Highfield for guests beyond the house. Wedded Concierge arranges those stays and times each transfer to the occasion it serves.

**Where does the legal ceremony fit in a destination wedding weekend?**
Hold it on site where the law allows, or schedule it before the celebration at a town hall or registry. Heckfield Place lists six spaces licensed for wedding ceremonies and can arrange a ceremony at nearby Heckfield Church, while Dove Cameron and Damiano David held their civil ceremony in Montalcino before the main ceremony at Castiglion del Bosco. For Italy, our [complete legal guide to marrying in Italy](https://weddedwonderland.com/expert-advice/getting-married-in-italy-the-complete-legal-guide-for-destination-couples), our look at [the second wedding era](https://weddedwonderland.com/trending/angel-dei-said-i-do-again) and our guide to [which Amalfi Coast venues you can marry in](https://weddedwonderland.com/expert-advice/which-amalfi-coast-venues-can-you-marry-in) set out the options.

**Can you hold a wedding weekend at a venue without exclusive use?**
Yes, for a smaller party, though the celebration then shares the property with other guests. Heckfield Place hosts weddings of up to 30 guests without exclusive use, per its Events 2025 brochure. Ask which spaces your party holds privately, and for how many hours, before you plan each occasion around them.

**Can a wedding weekend venue meet every guest's dietary and family needs?**
Ask at the first enquiry, because some kitchens and houses set limits of their own. Babylonstoren's 2026-27 wedding guide states that it cannot provide strictly halaal or kosher catering and that its Farmhouse is for adults only. Put every requirement to the events team in writing so the answer is part of your terms.

**How does the season change what a wedding weekend venue can offer?**
The season decides which gardens, terraces and lawns can carry an occasion and which need an indoor alternative. Babylonstoren's high season runs September to April and its low season May to August. Ask each venue which of its spaces it recommends for your month.

**Do you need a planner for a wedding weekend held at one venue?**
For a weekend with several occasions, a planner earns their place, because one property still means several suppliers, approvals and timings. Heckfield Place requires outside contractors to be approved, so every florist, band and photographer needs clearing with the venue in advance. Wedded Concierge coordinates suppliers across the weekend, and [Wonderland Studio](https://weddedwonderland.com/wonderland-studio) keeps the plan in one place.

**How many nights should you book a wedding weekend venue for?**
Book three nights for most weekends abroad, which gives guests an arrival night and then a night each for the welcome party and the wedding before a farewell brunch on the final morning. Check the venue's own minimum stay for exclusive use, covered under house rules above, since some set a floor of their own. For [how the days of a wedding weekend fit together](https://weddedwonderland.com/a-wedding-weekend-itinerary-for-guest-experience), our schedule guide sets them out in turn.
