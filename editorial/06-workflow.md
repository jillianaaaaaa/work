# 06 The Production Workflow

One end-to-end system. Efficiency comes from fewer unnecessary decisions, less repeated research and fewer avoidable corrections, not from publishing more.

## Seven stages

| # | Stage | What happens | Owner | Exit criterion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Plan | Assign audience, objective, content type, keyword intent, tier, priority and owner | Editorial lead (`ww-editorial-director`), with `ww-seo-strategist` | `status.md` complete; topic approved for the agenda |
| 02 | Research | Gather verified facts, sources, images, expert input, destination intelligence | `ww-research-intelligence` | `research.md` with sources and verified dates; open questions listed |
| 03 | Brief | Define the angle, structure, key information, tone, internal links and required evidence | `ww-writer` with `ww-seo-strategist` | `brief.md` + Checkpoint 1 outline approved by Jillian |
| 04 | Produce | Draft and edit against the approved brief | `ww-writer` | `draft.md` passes the skill's pre-delivery checklist |
| 05 | Review | Check facts, judgement, voice, originality, SEO, visual suitability | `ww-editorial-director` (+ SEO and fact review for Tier 1) | `review.md` verdict; Jillian or delegated editor approves (Checkpoint 2) |
| 06 | Publish | CMS fields, image rights and credits, metadata, mobile checks, links, structured data | `ww-publishing-ops` | `publish.md` with no unresolved items |
| 07 | Learn | Monitor results, record what worked, schedule updates or repurposing | `ww-seo-strategist` | Logged in `knowledge/performance-log.md` with a refresh date |

Drafting never begins before the brief is approved. Strategy is decided in the brief, not improvised in the draft.

## Asset folders

Each asset lives in `content/<slug>/`:

```
status.md     stage, owner, tier, classification, decisions, change log
research.md   stage 02
brief.md      stage 03 (and the Checkpoint 1 outline)
draft.md      stage 04
review.md     stage 05
publish.md    stage 06
```

`content/PIPELINE.md` lists every live asset with its stage and owner, so the weekly meeting does not need to read out a tracker.

## Review tiers

Not every caption needs the scrutiny of a flagship destination guide.

| Tier | Covers | Review |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1: Flagship authority** | Destination hubs, strategic features, major reports, high-value evergreen guides | Full editorial, factual and SEO review. Every fact traced to source. Jillian approves. |
| **Tier 2: Standard editorial** | Listings, trend articles, destination features | Established template, targeted editorial review (judgement, voice, new facts, links). Delegated editor may approve. |
| **Tier 3: Repeatable** | Social adaptations, routine updates, standard partner deliverables | Approved templates and delegated sign-off. Escalate to Tier 2 for any new claim, superlative, legal or fee detail, or material change. |

This keeps oversight without a bottleneck at the editor-in-chief's desk.

## Escalation

Escalate to the editorial lead when: a partner asks for a claim we cannot verify; agents disagree on angle or classification; a correction is needed on a published asset; a Tier 3 item contains a new claim; or a piece cannot answer the three questions.
