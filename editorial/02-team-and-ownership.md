# 02 Team and Ownership

The team is structured around functions with accountable owners, so no one is expected to research, strategise, write, edit, optimise, publish and distribute everything at once.

## Functions

| Function | Agent | Human owner | Accountable for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Editorial Director / Editor-in-Chief | `ww-editorial-director` | Jillian (editorial lead) | Standards, the brand's taste, content agenda approval, final judgement calls, protecting authority |
| Editorial Research & Intelligence | `ww-research-intelligence` | Assigned researcher | Destination research, trend monitoring, venue discovery, source verification, industry relationships |
| Editorial Production | `ww-writer` | Assigned writer | Briefs, features, listings, interviews, destination guides, copywriting, substantive editing |
| SEO & Content Strategy | `ww-seo-strategist` | SEO / content strategist | Search intent, destination architecture, internal linking, refreshes, performance analysis |
| Visual & Publishing Operations | `ww-publishing-ops` | Publishing editor | Image selection, rights and credits, CMS, metadata, QA, distribution |

Fill the human owner column as people are assigned. In a small team one person can hold several functions, but **editorial direction, final approval and publishing accountability must each be explicitly named**.

## Stage ownership

| Stage | Accountable owner |
|---|---|
| Editorial priority | Editorial lead |
| Research and verification | Researcher or assigned writer |
| Draft and optimisation | Writer / SEO editor |
| Editorial sign-off | Editor or delegated senior editor |
| CMS and publishing QA | Publishing editor |
| Performance and updates | SEO / content strategist |

Every asset has one named owner at every stage, recorded in `content/<slug>/status.md`. When a stage changes, the owner field changes with it.

## The editorial lead

The most important appointment. The lead does not need to write every article. They need the judgement to recognise exceptional material, develop other writers, decide consistently and hold standards under pressure. Capable writers without a strong decision-maker produce competent but interchangeable content.

## How the agents and humans work together

- Agents prepare, research, draft, check and recommend. Humans approve.
- `ww-editorial-director` writes a verdict and reasons; the editorial lead or delegated editor makes the call.
- Disagreements between agents (for example SEO wants a keyword-led angle the director considers generic) are written into `status.md` and escalated to the editorial lead. Taste wins over keyword placement; reader value decides close calls.
