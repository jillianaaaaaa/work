# Wedded Wonderland: Briefing

_Compiled 12 October 2026 from the WeddedAdmin platform data (aggregates only) and public web pages. Unless stated otherwise, figures cover the last 90 days (14 Jul 2026 to 12 Oct 2026) and are compared with the 90 days before that. Test and staff accounts are excluded._

---

## 1. What Wedded Wonderland is

- **Website:** [weddedwonderland.com](https://weddedwonderland.com)
- **Positioning:** A global B2B2C wedding platform for luxury, cultural and destination weddings. It links couples with venues, planners and wedding professionals in 54+ countries.
- **Founder:** Wendy El-Khoury (quoted on the About page).
- **History:** Older site material says education and consultation was the first product, launched in 2012. The bridal event now called Wedded World has run since at least 2019. The *Get Wedded* digital magazine series (destination planning guides by region) began with *Get Wedded in Australia* in June 2023.
- **Home market:** Australia, with a strong secondary audience in the US and Canada. Most subscriptions are billed in USD and most events in AUD.

## 9. Key takeaways

1. **The audience is growing fast but revenue fell.** Visitors are up 133%, contacts up 472% and form entries up 286%. Revenue is down 90% or more, because the previous quarter had a large membership sales push.
2. **Giveaways build the list.** They work for volume, but consent is low (335 of 2,206 contacts), so turning entrants into customers is the gap.
3. **Wonderland Studio has an activation problem.** Only 26% of new couples use a tool and only 4% use three or more. Couples who arrive from Instagram activate four times better than direct sign-ups. The Website Builder, Guest List, Seating Planner and Vendor Manager all lose most couples who open them.
4. **Wedded World Melbourne 2026 is the main revenue driver this quarter** (112 GA tickets and 3 buyer passes so far).
5. **Popups are not converting** (0.1% CTR) and are worth a redesign or retiring.
6. **The affiliate programme now drives traffic but few sales.** Partner activation and fraud checks (42 suspicious clicks, 10 rejected conversions) need attention.
7. **Data quality:** 663 contacts have no country, and Australia is stored as both "Australia" and "AU". 48 Studio sign-ups have no recorded surface.
