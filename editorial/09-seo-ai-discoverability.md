# 09 SEO, Editorial Judgement and AI Discoverability

Search optimisation strengthens the editorial product. It does not dictate taste.

## The reader journey

| Stage | The reader wants to establish | Typical assets |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Which destinations suit a particular celebration? | Region hubs, "best destinations for" guides, trend features |
| Comparison | How do locations, venues and travel arrangements differ? | Country and area guides, comparison features |
| Planning | Seasonal, legal, accommodation and logistical considerations | Planning guides, legal and seasonal explainers, dossiers turned into guides |
| Selection | Which properties or professionals meet our requirements? | Listings, curated shortlists, Signature features |
| Action | Where to explore further or connect with the provider | Listings, Wedded Concierge, packages pages |

## One primary purpose per strategic topic

For every strategic topic, assign one primary page and map supporting articles and listings to it in `knowledge/topic-map.md`. This prevents internal competition between similar pages and builds a connected knowledge base. Before planning a new asset, `ww-seo-strategist` checks the topic map and live rankings for overlap.

## Priorities for traditional and AI-driven search

- Original editorial judgement and credible expert contributions.
- Accurate, specific, consistently structured information.
- Clear explanations of destination suitability and practical constraints.
- Strong internal links between region guides, country pages, area guides and listings.
- Named sources and current verification for important claims.
- Helpful answers written for people, supported by sound indexing and structured data.

No practice guarantees inclusion in an AI-generated answer. The goal is content useful, credible, specific and well organised enough to merit discovery and citation.

## What we do not do

- Create an article only to target a keyword when it gives the reader nothing new.
- Let keyword placement override voice or accuracy.
- Publish near-duplicates of live pages; we update or consolidate instead.

Article-level SEO rules (keyword density, meta lengths, headings, links, schema, refresh loop) are in the `wedded-wonderland-articles` skill.
