Produce or update a listing for: $ARGUMENTS

1. `ww-research-intelligence` gathers and verifies the structured facts (capacities, exclusivity, ceremony options, season, restrictions, access, official links), each with source and verified date, and completes `templates/taste-assessment.md`.
2. `ww-editorial-director` assigns Signature, Specialist, Emerging or Decline with a reason. If Decline, record it in the relevant dossier and stop.
3. `ww-writer` writes the editorial copy to the six-part structure in `editorial/08-listing-standard.md`, using only verified fields.
4. Save as `knowledge/listings/<slug>.md` from `templates/listing-record.md` and add it to the dossier's venue ecosystem.
5. Tier 2 review by `ww-editorial-director`; list any unconfirmed details and whether disclosure is required.
