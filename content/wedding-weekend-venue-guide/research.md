## 2. What makes a property right for a whole wedding weekend

These are the venue-reading criteria the article can teach, each grounded in what operators actually publish. They describe questions, not judgements on any property.

| Criterion | What to ask the venue | Primary-source evidence (examples) |
|---|---|---|
| **Exclusive use, and what it really covers** | Is a full buy-out offered, or only partial? What stays open to the public (spa, restaurants, members, golf)? Is there a minimum stay? | Heckfield Place sells three tiers: "A Key to the House", "A Key to the House and Walled Gardens" (members still use The Bothy) and "A Key to Heckfield", the only tier with "total privacy"; two-night minimum; the farm is never included (Events 2025 PDF). Four Seasons separates "exclusives" (no outside use) from "buyouts", where restaurants, spa or golf may stay open to outside guests (group-wide definition surfaced via search, not read on a Lanai page: **unconfirmed** for Lanai). Castiglion del Bosco's weddings page refers to "large wedding parties requiring exclusive use of the whole estate". San Ysidro Ranch lists "Buyouts, up to 150 guests". |
| **Number of distinct settings, one per occasion** | Can the welcome dinner, ceremony, reception, after-party and day-after lunch each have a different space, without turning one room over overnight? | Villa Igiea publishes seven reception rooms with separate capacities. Heckfield lists six spaces licensed for ceremonies plus marquee lawns. Babylonstoren has three barns used in sequence (dinner in the Old Cellar, dessert in the White Studio, dancing in the Wheat Barn). Four Seasons Lanai suggests NOBU LANAI for "a private post-wedding brunch". |
| **Beds on site, and the gap between guests and beds** | How many guests can sleep on the property? What happens to the rest, and how far away are they? | Heckfield: up to 150 guests by day, 90 overnight; 28 more rooms at Wellington Lodge at Highfield can be arranged. Babylonstoren: the Farmhouse (nine bedrooms, adults only) and Koornhuis cottage come with the evening package; Old Cellar seats 100. This gap is the most useful single test for the reader. |
| **Movement between events** | Can guests walk between every occasion? If not, who runs transfers, and in what vehicles? | Castiglion del Bosco's eleven villas each come with a Land Rover Defender or similar 4x4, and the estate covers 5,000 acres (2,000 hectares). Heckfield offers Land Rovers or coaches. Four Seasons Lanai arranges airport and harbour transfers and says guests can be expected to stay on property, as Lanai has two resorts, both Four Seasons. |
| **Kitchens and dining formats** | How many kitchens can cater at once? Is there a restaurant that can be taken privately for the rehearsal or welcome dinner? Long table, family style, plated? | Heckfield: Skye Gyngell menus across Marle (seats 85 inside plus 40 on the terrace, exclusive use only), Hearth (open-fire cooking) and the Dining Room (26). Babylonstoren: family-style platters drawn from its own garden. Castiglion del Bosco: Michelin-starred Campo del Drago plus trattoria-style Osteria La Canonica. Four Seasons Lanai: Malibu Farm and VIEWS can be bought out; ONE FORTY and NOBU LANAI have private rooms for up to 24. |
| **Restrictions that shape the itinerary** | Curfews, music limits, minimum stays, required planners, weather back-ups. | Babylonstoren: evening functions end at midnight, extendable to 02h00 at an hourly fee; no outside dance floor "unless all accommodation is taken"; no children in the Farmhouse (2026-27 guide). Four Seasons Lanai: more than 30 guests requires an outside professional planner; beach ceremonies standing only, not before 4.30 pm, subject to local permit (2024 brochure). Heckfield: two-night minimum for exclusive use; Pavilion ceremonies suited to spring to autumn only. |
| **Where the legal ceremony fits** | Can the legal ceremony happen on site, or must it be at a town hall, church or registry? How far away? | Four Seasons Lanai helps arrange the Hawaii marriage licence (licence fee stated as USD 65, "subject to change"; any fee in the article needs the skill disclaimer). Heckfield arranges a notary and ceremonies at nearby Heckfield Church; six spaces are licensed (English law). For Italy, link to our existing legal guide and Angel Dei "Second Wedding Era" section rather than restating the rules (status must-not). If one line is needed: the Australian Embassy Rome states Australians need an Atto Notorio and a Nulla Osta sworn in Italy (see open question Q7). |

**Expert or planner commentary:** no named, independent, non-competitor planner or estate events director was found on the record this pass. Search returned ALHI (a hotel sales organisation) guidance on buy-outs, but the page now returns 404, and the rest was venue or vendor marketing. The criteria above therefore rest on operator documents, which is a strength for verification but leaves the piece without a human voice. See section 4.

### 3.1 Castiglion del Bosco, A Rosewood Hotel (Montalcino, Tuscany, Italy)
Listing stub: `knowledge/listings/castiglion-del-bosco.md`

**Verified (Rosewood official pages, 21 Oct 2026)**

- 5,000-acre private estate in a "900-year-old borgo"; Val d'Orcia.
- Exclusive use: the weddings page says "large wedding parties requiring exclusive use of the whole estate" are catered for, with a proposal covering accommodation, catering, transport, set-ups and entertainment. **This updates the director's 21 Oct note** (the homepage alone said nothing on exclusive use; the weddings page does). Terms, minimum stay and capacity remain unconfirmed. https://www.rosewoodhotels.com/en/castiglion-del-bosco/events/weddings
- Wedding settings: Castle Ruins (grounds of the medieval castle in the borgo), San Michele Arcangelo Church (frescoes by Pietro Lorenzetti), private villas "for intimate wedding parties", Capanna Vineyard; event spaces page adds Il Granaio (glass greenhouse over the Val d'Orcia), the winery and Members' Cellar, the golf Clubhouse (Tom Weiskopf course), Ristorante Campo del Drago (Michelin-starred) and Osteria La Canonica. https://www.rosewoodhotels.com/en/castiglion-del-bosco/events/event-space-listing

**Provisional taste note:** Design integrity Strong (restored borgo, Lorenzetti frescoes). Distinctiveness Strong (a village-scale estate with its own church, castle ruins and Brunello winery). Experience Strong (many distinct settings across one estate). Cultural and local relevance Strong (Brunello, Val d'Orcia UNESCO landscape). ### 3.4 Babylonstoren (Simondium, Cape Winelands, South Africa)

**Provisional taste note:** Design Strong (restored Cape Dutch farmyard; the hotel lists Great Wine Capitals 2025 "#1 Architecture and Landscape"). Experience Present (one evening format, three spaces used in sequence; a multi-day itinerary needs the hotel's restaurants too). Cultural and local relevance Strong.
